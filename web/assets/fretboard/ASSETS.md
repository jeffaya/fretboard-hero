# Fretboard material

`ebony.webp`: 768×768, optimized photorealistic ebony material generated with the built-in imagegen tool. It contains wood only; strings, frets, diamonds, labels and notes remain calculated SVG geometry. Horizontal grain is rotated in portrait.

Prompt: orthographic macro photograph of dark satin-oiled black ebony; fine horizontal organic fibers and pores, neutral even light, no frets, strings, inlays, text, borders, perspective or painted neon.
