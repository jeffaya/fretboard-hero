### Description complète — 2223 / 4 000 caractères

```text
Get to know your four-string bass. Bass Fretboard Hero brings guided practice, visual learning tools, a fretboard quiz and a tuner together in one app.

BUILD A DAILY PRACTICE HABIT
Follow five exercises in one key: find root notes, play a scale, connect triads, practice rhythm and explore pentatonic patterns. Clear tablature, note names and degree labels help you understand what you are playing. Move through the steps at your own pace and repeat your routine.

CONNECT THE BASS FRETBOARD
Explore the five pentatonic positions, major and minor triads, inversions and arpeggios. Highlight individual degrees, isolate a position and connect patterns across four strings in standard E–A–D–G tuning.

PLAY WITH MICROPHONE FEEDBACK
Enable the microphone in Daily Routine to follow the highlighted notes and receive feedback as you play. Practice notes and triads with guided exercises, and work on rhythm with an adjustable tempo. You can also practice with the microphone turned off.

TEST YOUR KNOWLEDGE
Challenge yourself in a 60-second fretboard quiz. Find roots, thirds and fifths, build an accuracy streak and explore more of the neck as you progress.

FIND EVERY NOTE
Use the Fretboard Map to reveal notes across the four strings or focus on one note at a time. Connect note names, intervals and positions as you move between learning tools.

MAKE HARMONY MAKE SENSE
Explore the interactive Circle of Fifths, relative minor keys, diatonic chords and common progressions. See how keys and chords relate to each other.

TUNE BEFORE YOU PLAY
Use the built-in bass tuner for standard E–A–D–G tuning. Choose automatic string detection or select a string manually. The display shows whether the note is sharp, flat or in tune.

START FOR FREE
Explore the Learn modes and positions in A, the Circle of Fifths and the A minor Daily Routine. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks all keys and notes in this app for life. No subscription is required.

MICROPHONE PRIVACY
Microphone access is optional and is used for tuning and live practice feedback. Audio is processed on your device and is not recorded or uploaded by the app.

Built for bassists who want to know their notes, connect patterns and make regular practice easier.
```
